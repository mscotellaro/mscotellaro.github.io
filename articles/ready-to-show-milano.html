Back on the show floor. On my own this time.

Three days at Ready to Show in Milano this week.

A hundred plus factories on one floor, from Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Turkey, Egypt. The value of being there in person isn't in the data. That arrives by email either way. It's in the fabric hand before any swatch, in how a factory actually talks about its own capacity.

I used to walk these shows with a mandate already in hand. This time I build it walking. Different job, same floor.

Mario Scotellaro — Sourcing & Production Specialist, Knit & Woven. Based in Italy, working across South Asia and Turkey.
